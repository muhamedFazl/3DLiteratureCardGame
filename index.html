How to Play

The deck. 48 cards — a standard deck with the four 8s removed — split into 8 half-suits of 6: the minor (2–7) and major (9–A) half of each suit.

Teams. Six players alternate around the table, so your two teammates sit two seats away in each direction.

Asking. On your turn, ask one opponent for one specific card. You must already hold at least one card of that half-suit, and you must not hold the card you ask for. If they have it, it moves to you and you go again. If not, the turn passes to them.

Declaring. At any point on your turn you may claim a half-suit by naming which of your teammates holds each of its six cards. All six right — your team scores. One wrong — the other team scores. Either way those cards leave play.

Winning. First team past four half-suits takes the match.

Controls. Left-click a card to inspect it or an opponent to ask them. Right-drag orbits the table, scroll zooms.
